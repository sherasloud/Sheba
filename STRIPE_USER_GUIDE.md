# Adding Money with Stripe - User Guide

## 🎯 Quick Start

Your Sheba Chai app now supports secure card payments through **Stripe**! You can add money to your wallet using:

- **VISA**
- **Mastercard**  
- **Amex**

## 💳 How to Add Money

### Step 1: Open Add Money
- Tap the **"Add Money"** button on your home screen

### Step 2: Choose Payment Method
You'll see three options:
- **Bank To Sheba** - Transfer from bank account
- **Card To Sheba** - SSLCommerz payment gateway
- **Stripe Payment** ← Click this for secure card payment

### Step 3: Select Amount
Choose how much you want to add:
- Tk 500
- Tk 1,000
- Tk 2,500
- Tk 5,000
- Tk 10,000

### Step 4: Enter Card Details
A secure Stripe form will appear. Enter:
- Card number
- Expiry date (MM/YY)
- CVV (3 digits on back)
- Cardholder name

### Step 5: Complete Payment
- Click **"Pay"**
- Wait for confirmation
- Your balance updates automatically!

## ✅ Success Indicators

After payment:
- ✅ Green success checkmark appears
- ✅ Balance updates in real-time
- ✅ Transaction ID is displayed
- ✅ SMS notification sent (if configured)

## 🔐 Security

Your card information:
- ✅ **Never stored** on our servers
- ✅ **Never visible** to our team
- ✅ **Encrypted** in transit
- ✅ **Handled by Stripe** (PCI-DSS certified)

It's the same security used by:
- Major banks
- Stripe itself
- Thousands of businesses worldwide

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is my card information safe?
**A:** Yes! Stripe is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified (highest security level). Your card details are handled exclusively by Stripe's secure servers.

### Q: What if payment fails?
**A:** 
1. You'll see an error message
2. Your balance won't change
3. No money will be deducted
4. Try again or contact support

### Q: Can I use international cards?
**A:** Yes! Stripe accepts most international VISA/Mastercard/Amex cards. Some banks may require additional verification.

### Q: How long does it take?
**A:** Usually 10-30 seconds. Your balance updates automatically when confirmed.

### Q: Do you charge extra fees?
**A:** Stripe charges standard payment processing fees (typically 2-3%). This may be reflected in final amount.

### Q: Can I add more than Tk10,000?
**A:** Currently, maximum per transaction is Tk10,000. Contact support if you need larger amounts.

### Q: What if I need a refund?
**A:** Contact our support team with:
- Transaction ID
- Date and amount
- Reason for refund

Refunds typically process within 3-5 business days.

### Q: Can I save my card?
**A:** Not currently. For security, you'll enter card details each time.

## 📱 Troubleshooting

### "Payment Failed"
- Check card number, expiry, and CVV
- Ensure your bank allows online payments
- Try a different card
- Contact your bank

### "Insufficient Funds"
- Your card doesn't have enough balance
- Try a lower amount
- Check card balance with your bank

### "Card Declined"
Common reasons:
- Wrong expiry date or CVV
- Card not activated for international transactions
- Daily transaction limit exceeded
- Contact your bank to approve

### Balance Didn't Update
- Wait 30 seconds (usually instant)
- Refresh the app
- Check payment confirmation in email
- Contact support with transaction ID
